What each number means

1

Whole cash cycle. Eligible, invoiced, overdue, on hold — the money pipeline at a glance for Finance.

2

Eligible list. Placements whose contract condition is met, sorted by value. Nothing is billed before it should be.

3

Nothing typed. Client, candidate, joining date, fee and tax are pre-filled from the chain; pre-flight checks block a wrong bill.

4

Clean separation. Recruiters source and place; Finance bills and collects — but on one shared record, so no re-entry between teams.
