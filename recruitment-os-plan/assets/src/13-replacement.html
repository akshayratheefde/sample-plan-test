What each number means

1

One clear picture. A single placement's whole guarantee period on a line — joined, check-ins, window end, bill. No mental maths.

2

Money at risk, live. Every active placement with days left and fee exposed — so the team protects revenue, not just fills seats.

3

Proactive check-ins. Regular nudges surface unhappiness early, when it can still be fixed.

4

Zero manual admin. Early exit auto-creates the replacement job and pauses billing — nobody has to remember the rule.

Why it's automation, not a screen to work: recruiters and account managers just watch the exposure list; the timers and jobs run themselves.
