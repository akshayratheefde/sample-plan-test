What each number means

1

Identity, fixed where it matters. Name and who it represents are editable; “say it is an AI & ask to record” is locked on for compliance.

2

Language & voice. Hindi, English or Hinglish (switches mid-call), with voice and calling window per desk.

3

Pre-filled prompt. The system ships a safe, working prompt. The TL edits the wording but keeps the variables that fill in per candidate and role.

4

Questions the desk owns. Reorder, edit, toggle on/off or add — the same questions feed the call, the WhatsApp chat and the recruiter's own screening.

5

Guardrails. Safety rules are toggles; the critical ones (consent, opt-out) are locked and can't be disabled.

Versioned & approved: edits become a new draft that goes live only after Admin sign-off. Every change is logged (who, what, when).
One script, three places: the prompt and questions set here drive the Voice AI call, the WhatsApp screening chat and the recruiter's manual take-over — so every candidate is asked the same, approved things.
