What each number means

1

Three-step start. A recruiter only needs to remember three verbs: UploadMatchLaunch. Everything else the system does or suggests.

2

Today list. One merged to-do across every role and campaign. Each row has a single coloured next-action button — red = do now, amber = waiting, green = quick win.

3

Live counters. Update on their own as campaigns run and candidates move. No report to build, no sheet to refresh.

Future scope · BDE. The greyed Sales menu (lead capture, client onboarding, contract terms) belongs to the Business Development team and is planned for a later release. Recruiters do not need it to work today.
Why it is simple: seven menu items, one Today screen, the same card layout on every record, and forms that arrive pre-filled — so a brand-new recruiter is productive on day one.
