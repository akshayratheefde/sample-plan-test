What each number means

1

Fee preview. The moment an offer is entered, the expected fee is computed from the contract — the recruiter sees the value they're about to create.

2

Documents chase themselves. Each item shows Received / Verified / Missing; reminders go out until the checklist is complete.

3

Back-out radar. Silence, late counter-offers and notice-period delays raise a risk flag early, so the recruiter can act before it's too late.

4

Joining ladder. A friendly reminder sequence to the candidate, ending with a joining-day check from both candidate and client.
