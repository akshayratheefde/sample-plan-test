What each number means

1

Search and saved views. Find anyone by name, phone or skill. Save a filter set as a view, such as “Warehouse Gurgaon”, and reopen it in one click.

2

Filters you can see. Chips show what is applied. The filter bar has the controls: stage, location, experience, expected CTC and notice period, plus skills, source, owner, consent, last contacted, match score and the desk's custom fields.

3

A real sheet. Frozen name column and header, sort by any column, click a cell to edit. Values filled by the AI carry an “AI” or “check” chip until a person confirms.

4

Select, then act. Tick rows and start a campaign, add to a requirement, message on WhatsApp or assign an owner. No export and re-import.

Under the hood: a grid on the shared candidates table (AG Grid, per the technical plan). Filters run on system fields and the TL's custom fields. Recruiters see their own rows; Team Leads see the desk. Every edit is logged.
Why it matters: recruiters keep the sheet habit they already have, but the data is shared, de-duplicated and one click from a campaign.
