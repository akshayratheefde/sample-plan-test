What each number means

1

Three simple tabs. Fields (what you collect), Templates (a blank sheet to hand out), Team uploads (who sent what).

2

Standard fields are locked. Their meaning is fixed so billing, Smart Match and reports never drift — the “universal” half of the model.

3

Custom fields are yours. Add typed fields for how your desk works, mark any as required — the “flexible” half, with no developer.

4

Give out a template. Share a blank Excel/CSV that already matches your fields, so uploads map instantly.

5

Team visibility. Every recruiter's upload and its health, in one list — no chasing on WhatsApp.

The universal-yet-flexible model: locked system fields (Postgres) + a TL-owned typed custom-field registry (stored as indexed JSON). Everyone gets their format; the company keeps one clean dataset.
